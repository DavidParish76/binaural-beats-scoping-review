# Project Overview

Binaural Beats Scoping Review

## Lead Researcher

## Working Title

Binaural Auditory Beat Stimulation as a Noninvasive Neuromodulatory Tool: A Scoping Review of Mechanisms, Stimulation Parameters, and Translational Relevance for Motor Control and Rehabilitation

## Background

Binaural auditory beat stimulation (BABS) is a non-invasive auditory technique in which two pure tones of slightly different frequencies are presented separately to each ear. The difference in frequency between the two tones creates a perceived beat at that difference frequency. This beat is hypothesised to entrain cortical oscillations via frequency-following responses, making BABS a candidate noninvasive neuromodulatory intervention.

Despite a growing body of literature, the field is fragmented. Studies vary widely in stimulation parameters (carrier frequency, beat frequency, duration, intensity, delivery method), population, outcome domain, and methodological rigour. A structured scoping review is needed to map this evidence and identify where future motor-control and rehabilitation research should be directed.

## Core Rationale

This project is motivated by three converging needs:

1. **Evidence mapping**: The binaural beat literature spans EEG/neurophysiology, cognition, anxiety/mood, pain, sleep, motor control, and autonomic regulation, but no comprehensive evidence map exists across all domains.

2. 2. **Parameter standardisation**: There is no consensus on which stimulation parameters are effective for which outcomes. This review will characterise the parameter space used to date.
  
   3. 3. **Thesis translation**: The project directly informs a future Master's thesis on sonification of a sine-wave visual motor template. The review will identify what is known about auditory-motor coupling, motor-relevant binaural beat effects, and what methodological gaps the thesis can fill.
     
      4. ## Scope
     
      5. - **Inclusion**: Human participants, binaural beat stimulation, any outcome domain, any study design (RCT, crossover, quasi-experimental, observational).
         - - **Exclusion**: Animal studies, purely theoretical papers, monaural beats only, commercial wellness reports without empirical data.
           - - **No date restriction**; English language primary.
            
             - ## Methodological Framework
            
             - - PRISMA for Scoping Reviews (PRISMA-ScR)
               - - JBI methodology for scoping reviews
                 - - No meta-analysis unless a sufficiently homogeneous cluster emerges
