# master_search_strategy_v0.1

## Project

**Binaural Beats, Brainwave Entrainment, and Motor Control: A Scoping Review and Translational Framework**

## Primary Review Question

What stimulation parameters, mechanistic measures, and behavioral or clinical outcomes have been used in human studies of binaural auditory beat stimulation, and what evidence exists for translation into motor-control, autonomic regulation, and rehabilitation research?

---

## 1. Core Search Logic

The review search will use five conceptual blocks:

```text
Block A = Binaural beat / auditory beat stimulation terms
Block B = Brainwave entrainment / neurophysiological mechanism terms
Block C = Behavioral and clinical outcome terms
Block D = Motor-control and rehabilitation translation terms
Block E = Autonomic regulation terms
```

The broad master search combines:

```text
A AND (B OR C OR D OR E)
```

This keeps binaural beats or closely related auditory beat terminology as the required concept while allowing retrieval across mechanism, cognition, affect, pain, sleep, motor control, rehabilitation, and autonomic regulation.

---

## 2. Block A: Core Binaural / Auditory Beat Terms

Use this as the required concept block.

```text
"binaural beat*" OR "binaural auditory beat*" OR "binaural acoustic stimulation" OR "binaural beat stimulation" OR "binaural beats stimulation" OR "auditory beat stimulation" OR "auditory beats" OR "beat stimulation" OR "brainwave entrainment" OR "brain wave entrainment"
```

### Notes

This block intentionally includes broader terms such as **auditory beat stimulation** and **brainwave entrainment** because some relevant studies discuss binaural beats under broader auditory entrainment terminology.

During screening, exclude studies that do not include a clearly identifiable binaural beat or binaural auditory stimulation condition.

## 19. Search Execution Order

Run searches in this order:

1. PubMed broad search
2. PubMed mechanism-focused search
3. PubMed motor-relevant search
4. PubMed autonomic-relevant search
5. PsycINFO broad search
6. Embase broad search
7. Scopus or Web of Science broad search
8. Cochrane CENTRAL broad search
9. Backward citation chasing from key reviews
10. Forward citation chasing from key reviews and motor-relevant studies

---

## 20. Decision Rule for Search Revision

After the first PubMed broad search, record the number of results.

| Result count | Action |
|---:|---|
| Fewer than 50 | Search is too narrow; loosen outcome terms |
| 50–300 | Good for first-pass screening |
| 300–1000 | Acceptable for scoping review |
| More than 1000 | Consider splitting into domain-specific searches |
| More than 3000 | Search is too broad; require title/abstract binaural terms and domain filters |
