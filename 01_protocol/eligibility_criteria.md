# Eligibility Criteria

## Population

Include human participants of any age, including healthy and clinical populations.

## Intervention

Include studies using binaural beats, binaural auditory beat stimulation, binaural acoustic stimulation, binaural beat stimulation, auditory beat stimulation, or closely related beat-based auditory stimulation.

## Comparators

Eligible comparators include silence, no stimulation, sham stimulation, pure tone, white noise, pink noise, music, monaural beats, conventional acoustic stimulation, or alternative frequency conditions.

## Outcomes

Eligible outcomes include EEG, MEG, ASSR, FFR, neural entrainment, spectral power, connectivity, cognition, attention, memory, anxiety, mood, pain, sleep, motor learning, motor performance, tremor, rehabilitation outcomes, HRV, or other autonomic measures.

## Study Designs

Include randomized controlled trials, crossover studies, within-subject experimental studies, quasi-experimental studies, and observational intervention studies.

## Exclusion Criteria

Exclude:
- Nonhuman studies unless specifically retained for background only.
- - Purely theoretical papers.
  - - Commercial wellness reports without empirical data.
    - - Studies where binaural beat effects cannot be isolated from a bundled intervention.
      - - Case reports unless retained for rare clinical signal mapping.
        - - Review articles from primary extraction, though they may be used for background and citation chasing.
         
          - ## Language and Date
         
          - - No date restriction.
            - - English language primary.
              - - Other languages included if translation is available.
