# Screening Instructions

## Overview

This document provides instructions for title/abstract and full-text screening. Follow these instructions exactly. Do not deviate from the eligibility criteria without logging a decision.

## General Rules

- Screen independently before comparing decisions.
- - Resolve conflicts by discussion; if unresolved, escalate to lead reviewer.
  - - When in doubt at title/abstract stage, include (err toward inclusion).
    - - At full-text stage, apply eligibility criteria strictly and record reason for exclusion.
     
      - ## Title/Abstract Screening
     
      - **Include if:**
      - - The study involves binaural beats, binaural auditory beat stimulation, or brainwave entrainment.
        - - The study involves human participants.
          - - Any measurable outcome is reported.
           
            - **Exclude if:**
            - - The study is clearly animal research.
              - - The study is clearly a theoretical or commercial paper with no empirical data.
                - - Binaural beats are not the primary intervention and cannot be isolated.
                 
                  - **Decision options in Rayyan:**
                  - - Include
                    - - Exclude
                      - - Maybe (use sparingly; discuss with co-screener)
                       
                        - ## Full-Text Screening
                       
                        - Apply all eligibility criteria from `01_protocol/eligibility_criteria.md`.
                       
                        - Record reason for exclusion using codes from `03_screening/exclusion_reasons.md`.
                       
                        - ## Logging
                       
                        - - Title/abstract decisions: `03_screening/title_abstract_screening_log.csv`
                          - - Full-text decisions: `03_screening/full_text_screening_log.csv`
                            - - PRISMA counts: `03_screening/prisma_flow_counts.csv`
