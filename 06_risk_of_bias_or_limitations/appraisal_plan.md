# Methodological Appraisal Plan

## Position on Quality Appraisal

Per JBI methodology for scoping reviews, formal risk-of-bias assessment is not required. However, methodological limitations will be recorded for each study to contextualise the evidence map and inform the Discussion.

## Approach

For each included study, record the following 5-item methodological quality note:

1. Beat frequency reported (Yes / No)
2. 2. Carrier frequency reported (Yes / No)
   3. 3. Stimulation duration reported (Yes / No)
      4. 4. Control condition described (Yes / No)
         5. 5. Blinding described (Yes / No)
           
            6. A study reporting all 5 items = high methodological transparency.
            7. Studies missing 3 or more = low methodological transparency.

            9. This appraisal informs the Discussion on methodological heterogeneity, the Evidence Map notes, and thesis integration planning.
