# Thesis Relevance Scale

This scale rates how directly each study informs future work on sonification of the sine-wave visual motor template and related auditory-motor control research.

| Score | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 0 | Not relevant to thesis except broad background |
| 1 | Relevant to auditory neuromodulation generally |
| 2 | Relevant to auditory-motor or sensorimotor mechanisms |
| 3 | Directly relevant to motor learning, motor control, kinematics, tremor, motor performance, or rehabilitation |

## Guidance Notes

- Score 0: EEG studies, pure cognition studies, pain studies, and sleep studies that have no motor component receive this score unless they illuminate mechanism relevant to motor control.
- - Score 1: Studies measuring attention, executive function, or arousal that plausibly relate to motor preparedness or motor learning receive this score.
  - - Score 2: Studies involving sensorimotor integration, rhythm perception, auditory cueing, or any auditory-motor coupling mechanism receive this score.
    - - Score 3: Studies explicitly measuring motor outcomes (reaction time, kinematics, gait, balance, dexterity, grip strength, tremor, motor learning trials, rehabilitation outcomes) receive this score.
     
      - ## Thesis Linkage
     
      - A score of 3 indicates the study should be read and annotated in detail in `11_notes/article_notes/`. Scores of 2 may be relevant for the Discussion and the thesis bridge memo. Scores of 0-1 inform the background section only.
